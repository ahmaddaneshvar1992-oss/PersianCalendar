# Privacy Policy — Persian Calendar

Last updated: [insert date]

The "Persian Calendar" application ("we", "the app") is committed to protecting user privacy. This document explains what data the app holds and how it is used.

## 1. Data stored by the app

- **Events & notes:** All events, birthdays and notes you enter are stored **only on your device's local storage** and are never sent to any server.
- **Settings:** Your choices such as theme, accent color, city for prayer times and Persian digits are stored on the device.
- **Backup file:** Only when you explicitly request an export, a JSON backup file is created in a location you choose. Keeping or sharing that file is entirely up to you.

## 2. Permissions

- **Location (optional):** Used only to compute prayer times for your current position. Your location is **never stored or transmitted**. If you deny the permission, you can pick your city from the list and all features keep working.
- **Notifications:** To show event reminders and prayer-time notifications.
- **Exact alarms:** To fire reminders precisely on time.

## 3. Advertising

The app is free and uses **Google AdMob** as its advertising service. AdMob may use the device's Advertising ID and ad-interaction data to serve relevant ads. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

You can opt out of personalized ads from your Google account settings or Android's Ads settings.

## 4. Data the app does NOT collect

- Name, email, phone number or any identity data
- The contents of your events (there is no server)
- Location data (never stored or transmitted)

## 5. Data security

Because all your data stays on your device, there is no server-side leak risk from the app itself. Uninstalling the app removes all data permanently.

## 6. Changes to this policy

If this document is updated, the "last updated" date changes. Continued use of the app after changes means acceptance of the updated policy.
